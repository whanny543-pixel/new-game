How to play

Goal: destroy every enemy structure. Build a base, gather supplies, mass an army, and use your generals powers and superweapon.

  • Tap a unit or building to select it. Double-tap to select all of that type on screen.
  • Tap the ground with units selected to move. Tap an enemy to attack. Tap a supply pile with a gatherer to harvest.
  • Drag to pan the camera. Pinch to zoom. Hold, then drag to box-select units.
  • Select a Dozer (or Worker) and pick a structure from the bar, tap where to put it, then tap again to confirm.
  • Select a building to train units. Hold a unit button to cancel it from the queue. Set a rally point so new units gather where you want.
  • Power: Coalition and Dominion need reactors. Low power slows production and shuts down defences. The Insurgency needs none.
  • Infantry capture oil derricks and enemy buildings with the Capture button.
  • Generals powers live in the right column. Destroying things earns rank; spend points with the ★ button.
  • Control groups: tap a numbered chip to select, hold it to assign.
  • Keyboard (desktop): WASD pan, A attack-move, S stop, F select army, H home, 1–4 groups (Ctrl to assign), Esc cancel.
